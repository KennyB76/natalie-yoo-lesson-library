Trapezoid Two Ways  New 5/21
Drag $b_1$, $b_2$, $h$. Press Flip & Match to see two trapezoids form a parallelogram (Way A). Switch tabs, press Cut & Split to see the diagonal break it into two triangles (Way B). Both give $\dfrac{(b_1+b_2)h}{2}$.

A note

"Natalie — Today's big idea: area = counting unit squares. Every formula (triangle, parallelogram, trapezoid) is just a clever way to count those squares without drawing them all. For the trapezoid, there are TWO ways to see why the formula works — flip a copy into a parallelogram, or cut the diagonal into two triangles. Play with the explorer, then try the 20 questions. — Mr. K"
