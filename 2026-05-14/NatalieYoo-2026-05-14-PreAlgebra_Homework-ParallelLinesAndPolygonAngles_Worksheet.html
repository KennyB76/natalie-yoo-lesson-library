Part D — Polygon Basics (Q14–17)

Directions: Use the polygon name table and definitions below to answer each question.

Polygon names by number of sides:
3 = Triangle  |  4 = Quadrilateral  |  5 = Pentagon  |  6 = Hexagon  |  7 = Heptagon  |  8 = Octagon
9 = Nonagon  |  10 = Decagon  |  11 = Hendecagon  |  12 = Dodecagon  |  any \(n\) = \(n\)-gon

Convex: all interior angles < 180° (no "dents").
Concave: at least one interior angle > 180° (has a "dent" or caves in).
Regular: all sides equal and all angles equal.
Irregular: sides or angles are not all equal.
